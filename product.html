SCENT ENGINE

$54.99 Design concept

A conversation between a dog and its nose. One push selects a new scent, releases it into the air, and earns a small measured reward. No electricity. No app. No noise.

  • Mechanical nose paddle
  • Six interchangeable scents
  • Measured reward tray
  • Under 32 dB, always
Starting scent

All six cartridges are included. Rabbit sits in the carousel first.

Concept checkout — no payment is taken.

Inside the box

One SCENT ENGINE, six scent cartridges, one removable food tray, and a short concept manual. Assembled by hand in small batches — in another life.

How it works

Nose to paddle, the carousel turns, a new scent arrives. Food falls only when the dog earns it, in a portion small enough to matter. Swap any cartridge to change the conversation.

The honest note

SCENT ENGINE is a design concept. It has not been tested on dogs in real conditions, and no physical product exists yet. Every order here is part of the concept experience.
